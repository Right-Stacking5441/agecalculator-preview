## Body

This page explains what happens to the information you put into agecalculator.com. The short version: the calculators run in your browser and the dates and names you type stay on your device. We only use Google Analytics, to count visits and see which calculators are used, if you click Accept. If you reject it, or never choose, every calculator works exactly the same and nothing is sent to Google Analytics.

## The dates and names you type

Every calculation on this site is done by JavaScript in your browser. When you type a date of birth, a test date or your dog’s age, the result is worked out on your own device. The page doesn’t send what you type to us or to anyone else, including Google Analytics, and there are no accounts or forms that submit to a server.

The same goes for the optional names on the age difference calculator and the pupil names on the chronological age class sheet. They are only used to label the result on your screen. Close or reload the tab and they are gone.

## Cookies and storage on your device

Things the site needs. Your cookie choice is saved in your browser’s local storage so we don’t ask again on every page. No consent is needed for this because it’s strictly necessary to remember the choice you made. Nothing you type is saved, so it isn’t there next time you visit. The one small exception is if you press Accept after pressing Reject on the same page: the page reloads, and what you typed or chose is held in your browser’s temporary session storage for that one reload, then deleted.

Where you type your own date of birth, the box is marked as a birthday field, so your browser may offer to fill in a date it has saved for you. That autofill is kept by your browser, not by us.

Google Analytics cookies (only if you accept).

| Name | Set by | Purpose | Lasts |
|---|---|---|---|
| `_ga` | agecalculator.com (Google Analytics) | Tells one browser apart from another, so we can count visitors | Up to 2 years |
| `_ga_DQQ5G4H336` | agecalculator.com (Google Analytics) | Keeps track of your visit (session) | Up to 2 years |

Until you accept, these cookies aren’t set and the Google Analytics script isn’t loaded.

## What Google Analytics measures

If you accept, we measure which pages you visit and Google Analytics’ standard automatic measurements, such as scrolling, clicks on links to other sites and time on the page. Google Analytics also receives technical details: your browser and operating system and their main versions, your device type (such as phone or computer), your language setting, the page that sent you here, and your approximate location down to country and region, worked out from your IP address. We’ve switched off Google Analytics’ collection of city-level location and detailed device information, such as your device’s brand and model and your screen resolution. Google says Google Analytics 4 does not log or store IP addresses.

We never send Google the dates or names you type or the results you get. Where a date is in the web address, such as /?dob=15 March 1995 or /past/1990/, we remove it before the page address is sent. We can’t see who you are. We look at the results only as totals, to understand how the site is used and make it better.

We have turned off Google Signals and all of Google Analytics’ data-sharing settings, and the site tells Google Analytics not to use your data for advertising or ads personalisation. Google uses this data only to provide and maintain the Analytics service for us. We haven’t linked Google Analytics to any other Google product, such as Google Ads.

## Our legal basis

We use Google Analytics only with your consent (regulation 6 of the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations 2003 and, for visitors in the EU, the ePrivacy rules where they live). The personal data in it is processed on the basis of that consent (UK GDPR and EU GDPR Article 6(1)(a)). UK law now allows some analytics without consent, but we ask everyone, wherever they are.

## Changing your mind

You can withdraw consent at any time: open Cookie settings in the footer at the bottom of this page or any calculator page and press Reject. That switches analytics off straight away and deletes the Google Analytics cookies this site set. Withdrawing doesn’t affect anything measured before you withdrew. You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Calculators embedded on other websites never use analytics, so there’s nothing to switch off there.

## Links with dates in them

Some pages can be filled in from the web address, for example `/?dob=15 March 1995`, `/past/1990/` or `?tz=Europe/London` to choose the time zone used for “today”. The page reads these in your browser in the same way as anything you type.

Bear in mind that anything in a web address is part of the request your browser sends for the page, is kept in your browser history, and is visible to anyone you share the link with. If you’d rather a date of birth didn’t appear in any of those places, type it into the box instead of using a link.

## Fonts, scripts and outside services

The font, the stylesheet and our own scripts all come from agecalculator.com itself. The only script from another company is Google Analytics, and it only loads if you click Accept. The site doesn’t use tracking pixels, social media buttons, or fonts from other companies.

Some pages link to outside sources, such as the American Animal Hospital Association, legislation.gov.uk and news sites. If you follow one of those links you are on their site, under their privacy policy.

## Copy and print buttons

The Copy, Copy details, Copy link (Share on phones) and Print buttons on the calculators put the result on your clipboard, open your phone’s share sheet, or open your browser’s own print dialog, only when you press them. None of them sends anything to us. If you share a result, it goes to whichever app or person you choose, under their privacy policy.

## The calculator on other websites

Other sites can put our age calculator on their pages. It works the same way there: the dates are worked out in the visitor’s browser and aren’t sent to us. So that the frame can grow to fit the result, the calculator tells the page it sits on how tall it is, and nothing else. The site you are visiting has its own privacy policy. Google Analytics doesn’t run in the calculator when it’s on another site.

## How long we keep it

Google Analytics keeps event-level and user-level data for 2 months, then deletes it automatically in its next monthly clean-up. The totals and reports we’ve already produced may be kept longer, but they don’t identify anyone. The cookies above last up to 2 years (some browsers delete them sooner) unless you delete them or withdraw consent.

## Children

People of all ages use this site, and teachers use the chronological age calculator for pupils. Analytics stays off unless someone clicks Accept, and the calculators never send what you type to anyone. If you’re under 13, please ask a parent or carer before clicking Accept.
