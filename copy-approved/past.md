## Body

## How to find your age on a past date

Type your date of birth, then the date you want to look back to, and the page answers "how old was I" for that day: completed years in the big number, then the months and days since the birthday before it. The result also shows the birthday that came next after that date, how many days you had lived by then, and how long ago the date was.

It works for any date from your birth onwards. If the date you type is before you were born, the page tells you how many days early it is rather than showing a negative age.

## When people need their age on a past date

Forms ask this more than people do. "Age at the date of the incident", "age when employment began", "age at diagnosis", "age on the date of marriage". They nearly always mean completed years on that day, which is the big number. If the form also wants months, they are on the line underneath.

It also settles smaller questions: how old you were when you passed your driving test, when a grandparent died, or when a photo was taken.

## How old was I? A worked example

Someone born on 8 August 1987 wants to know how old they were on 23 June 2016, the day of the EU referendum.

- 2016 minus 1987 is 29, but their 2016 birthday (8 August) hadn't happened yet on 23 June, so it is 28 completed years, counted from 8 August 2015.
- 8 August 2015 to 8 June 2016 is 10 whole months.
- 8 June to 23 June is 15 days.

So they were 28 years, 10 months and 15 days old, and they turned 29 about six weeks later, on 8 August 2016. The quick check is the first line: subtract the years, then take one off if the birthday hadn't come round yet.

## Coming from an old year link?

Older versions of this site had a separate page for each year, such as "how old was I in 1990". A year isn't a single day, and your age changes partway through it. Links like that now open this page with the date set to 1 January of that year, and a note above the result says so. Change it to the day you actually mean.

If you only know the year, the answer is one of two ages. Before your birthday in that year you were the year minus your birth year, minus one. From your birthday onwards you were one older.

## Leap days and month ends

This calculator counts a 29 February birthday as falling on 1 March in common years. So someone born on 29 February 1996 turned 5 on 1 March 2001, and on 28 February 2001 they were still 4. A month after 31 January is 1 March for the same reason: February has no 31st. That's the rule the main [age calculator](/) uses, so the two pages always agree.

## Which day is "today"

You don't have to look back. Leave the date as today and this page works like the homepage. Today's date comes from your device's time zone, which you can change under Time zone. For dates still to come, [how old will I be](/how-old-will-i-be) is set up for future years.

## Past age FAQs

### How old was I in 1990?
It depends on whether your birthday had come yet. Before your birthday in 1990 you were 1990 minus your birth year, minus one. From your birthday on, you were one older. Type your date of birth and a date in 1990 to see the exact figure.

### How do I work out my age on a specific date by hand?
Subtract your birth year from the year of the date. If your birthday hadn't happened yet that year, take one off. Then count the whole months and days since your last birthday.

### What age do I put on a form that asks for age at a past date?
Usually the completed years on that day, the big number here. Use months as well only if the form asks for them.

### Does it work for dates before 1960?
Yes. Any date from the year 1000 to 2999 can be typed in, as long as it is after the date of birth.
