Privacy

Last updated [PLACEHOLDER: date this policy is published]

This page explains what happens to the information you put into agecalculator.com. The short version: the calculators run in your browser, the dates you type stay on your device, and the site doesn’t use cookies, analytics or anything from other companies.

Who runs this site

agecalculator.com is run by Hurrell Capital Limited, a company registered in England and Wales (company number 15158828), registered office 65 London Road, Stapleford, Cambridge, CB22 5DG. If you have a question about this policy, email hello@agecalculator.com.

The dates and names you type

Every calculation on this site is done by JavaScript in your browser. When you type a date of birth, a test date or your dog’s age, the result is worked out on your own device. The page doesn’t send what you type to us or to anyone else, and there are no accounts or forms that submit to a server.

The same goes for the optional names on the age difference calculator and the pupil names on the chronological age class sheet. They are only used to label the result on your screen. Close or reload the tab and they are gone.

Cookies and storage on your device

The site doesn’t set cookies. It doesn’t save anything in your browser’s local storage or session storage either, so nothing you type is still there next time you visit.

Where you type your own date of birth, the box is marked as a birthday field, so your browser may offer to fill in a date it has saved for you. That autofill is kept by your browser, not by us.

Links with dates in them

Some pages can be filled in from the web address, for example /?dob=15 March 1995, /past?year=1990 or ?tz=Europe/London to choose the time zone used for “today”. The page reads these in your browser in the same way as anything you type.

Bear in mind that anything in a web address is part of the request your browser sends for the page, is kept in your browser history, and is visible to anyone you share the link with. If you’d rather a date of birth didn’t appear in any of those places, type it into the box instead of using a link.

What our hosting provider receives

These pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. According to GitHub’s own documentation, when a GitHub Pages site is visited, the visitor’s IP address is logged and stored for security purposes, whether or not the visitor is signed in to GitHub.

Fonts, scripts and outside services

The font, the scripts and the stylesheet all come from agecalculator.com itself. The site doesn’t load analytics, tracking pixels, social media buttons, or fonts and scripts from other companies.

Some pages link to outside sources, such as the American Animal Hospital Association, legislation.gov.uk and news sites. If you follow one of those links you are on their site, under their privacy policy.

Copy and print buttons

The Copy buttons on the chronological age page put the result on your clipboard only when you press them. Print uses your browser’s own print dialog. Neither sends anything to us.

The calculator on other websites

Other sites can put our age calculator on their pages. It works the same way there: the dates are worked out in the visitor’s browser and aren’t sent to us. So that the frame can grow to fit the result, the calculator tells the page it sits on how tall it is, and nothing else. The site you are visiting has its own privacy policy.

Changes to this policy

If the way the site handles information changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
