What year was I born? Why there can be two answers

An age alone doesn’t pin down a birth year. During any year, people who share an age were born in one of two years, depending on whether their birthday has come round yet.

The sum is short. Take the current year and subtract the age. If the birthday has already happened this year, that is the birth year. If it hasn’t, take one more off.

A worked example

As of 16 November 2026, someone who tells you they are 31 was born in 1995 or 1994:

  • 2026 minus 31 is 1995.
  • If they’ve had their birthday this year, they were born in 1995.
  • If their birthday is still to come (some time between 17 November and 31 December), they were born in 1994.

So “I’m 31” in mid-November 2026 means 1995 for most people, and 1994 only for someone with a birthday in the last six weeks of the year. Choose “Not sure” and the page shows 1994 or 1995.

Working from an age in a past year

You don’t have to use today. Press Change, enter the date the age applies to, and the page does the same sum for that year. Someone who was 18 on 1 June 2010 and had already had their birthday that year was born in 1992. If their birthday was later in the year, it was 1991.

This is handy with old school reports, newspaper cuttings or forms that give an age without a date of birth. A news story from 1990 that calls someone “42” places their birth in 1947 or 1948.

Leap-day and New Year birthdays

This calculator counts a 29 February birthday as falling on 1 March in common years, so in 2026 the answer to “birthday been yet?” turns to Yes on 1 March. Someone born on 1 January has always had their birthday by the time anyone asks, so for them there is only one answer: the current year minus their age.

The tool deals in whole years only. For exact months and days between two dates, days between dates will count them.

Questions people ask

What year was I born if I’m 31?

As of 16 November 2026, 1995 if you’ve had your birthday this year, or 1994 if it’s still to come.

How do I work out my birth year from my age?

Subtract your age from the current year. If your birthday hasn’t happened yet this year, take one more off.

Why does the calculator give two years?

Because you chose “Not sure”. Without knowing whether the birthday has passed, an age fits two birth years. Answer Yes or Not yet to get one.

Can I work out a birth year from an age in the past?

Yes. Press Change, enter the date the age applied to, and the calculator works from that year instead of this one.
