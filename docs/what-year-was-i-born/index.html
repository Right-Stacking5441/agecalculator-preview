What year was I born?

Type an age in whole years, and the date on which that age is true (today, unless you change it). The result is a window of possible dates of birth, and the year or years they fall in.

Defaults to today in your time zone.

Possible birth years

Why the answer is usually two years

Age in whole years is not enough to pin down a single date of birth. Someone who is 31 years old on 16 November 2026 could have been born as early as 17 November 1994 (they turn 32 tomorrow) or as late as 16 November 1995 (they turned 31 today). So the birth year is 1994 or 1995. That is not vagueness in the tool; it is the information you actually typed.

If you also know the birthday, use the age calculator instead, and you will get an exact age rather than a window. If you know the age on a date in a particular year — “I was 10 on 1 January 2005” — change the date field to that day.

The edges of the window use the same 1 March convention for a 29 February anniversary in a common year as the rest of the site.
