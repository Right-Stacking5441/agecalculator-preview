How the countdown is counted

The next birthday is the next civil anniversary on or after the “count from” date. For most dates of birth that is the same month and day in this year or next year. For 29 February, it is 29 February in a leap year and 1 March in a common year.

From 16 November 2026, someone born on 15 March next turns a year older on 15 March 2027 — 119 days later. Someone born on 29 February next turns a year older on 1 March 2027, because 2027 is not a leap year.

The homepage also shows this countdown under the age result. This page exists for people who only want the wait, not the age itself. For age on a future date (“how old will I be in 2050”), use How old will I be?.

Questions people actually ask

When is the next birthday if I was born on 29 February?

29 February in a leap year, and 1 March in a common year, which is the same civil rule as the age calculator.

Does the count include today?

If today is the birthday, the countdown is 0 days. Otherwise it is the number of midnights until the next anniversary.
