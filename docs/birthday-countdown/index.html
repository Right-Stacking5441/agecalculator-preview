Birthday countdown

For cards, parties and plain impatience: the days left until the next birthday, and the day of the week it lands on.

Whose birthday? Type the date of birth to start counting.

From 16 November to 15 March

On Monday 16 November 2026, for example, someone born on 15 March 1995 has 119 days to go until their next birthday. Counted out:

  • Days left in November after the 16th: 14.
  • December: 31. January: 31. February 2027: 28, because 2027 isn’t a leap year.
  • Days in March up to the 15th: 15.

14 + 31 + 31 + 28 + 15 = 119 days, which is exactly 17 weeks. That birthday falls on Monday 15 March 2027, when they turn 32.

What the countdown shows

The big number is the days left until the next birthday, with the date, the weekday and the age it brings underneath. Under that you’ll find the same wait in weeks and days, and a clock counting down to midnight tonight. Open More to see whether the birthday lands on a weekend and how many birthdays have gone by so far. The clock is the only part of the page that moves.

If the birthday is today, the page says so and wishes a happy birthday, instead of counting to next year.

Which day it counts to

The big number is whole calendar days from today to the birthday. On the day before, it says 1. Today’s date comes from your time zone, so if you’re in London and the birthday person is in Sydney, their birthday will start nine to eleven hours before yours does, depending on the time of year, and this page won’t show it until it’s the day where you are.

The “until midnight tonight” line ticks down the rest of today in your time zone. On the day before a birthday, the big number says 1 and the clock shows exactly how long is left.

Born on 29 February

Born on 29 February? In years without one, the countdown runs to 1 March, and a note on the result says so (here’s why). On 16 November 2026, for example, someone born on 29 February 2008 has 105 days to go, to Monday 1 March 2027, when they turn 19. In 2028 the countdown runs to 29 February itself. This is the same rule the main age calculator uses, so the two pages always agree on the date.

Counting down for someone else

Type their date of birth instead of yours. The result shows the age they’re turning, which is handy for the number on the card or the candles, and the weekday, which helps when you’re planning a party around it. To see how much older or younger they are than you, use the age difference calculator.

What the other figures mean

The line under the date splits the days left into whole weeks and leftover days. Under More, Weekend birthday? tells you whether the date is a Saturday or Sunday, and Birthdays so far is the number of birthdays already had, which is the same as current age in completed years.

To count down to something that isn’t a birthday, such as a wedding, a due date or the end of term, put today and that date into days between dates. It gives the same day count plus weekdays.
