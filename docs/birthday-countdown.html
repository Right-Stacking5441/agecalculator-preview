Type a date and the result appears here as you go.

Type a date of birth and the birthday countdown shows how many days are left until the next birthday, the date and weekday it falls on, and the age it brings. Under that you’ll find the same wait in weeks, whether the birthday lands on a weekend, how many birthdays have gone by so far, and a clock counting down to midnight tonight. The clock is the only part of the page that moves.

If the birthday is today, the page says so and wishes a happy birthday, instead of counting to next year.

How the birthday countdown counts

The big number is whole calendar days from today to the birthday. On the day before, it says 1. Today’s date comes from your time zone, so if you’re in London and the birthday person is in Sydney, their birthday will start nine to eleven hours before yours does, depending on the time of year, and this page won’t show it until it’s the day where you are.

The “until midnight tonight” line ticks down the rest of today in your time zone. On the day before a birthday, the big number says 1 and the clock shows exactly how long is left.

A worked example

As of Monday 16 November 2026, someone born on 15 March 1995 has 119 days to go until their next birthday. Counted out:

  • Days left in November after the 16th: 14.
  • December: 31. January: 31. February 2027: 28, because 2027 isn’t a leap year.
  • Days in March up to the 15th: 15.

14 + 31 + 31 + 28 + 15 = 119 days, which is exactly 17 weeks. Their birthday falls on Monday 15 March 2027, when they turn 32. It isn’t a weekend birthday this time.

Born on 29 February

This calculator counts a 29 February birthday as falling on 1 March in common years, so in a year without 29 February the countdown runs to 1 March, and a note on the result says so. That is the rule in Scotland under the Age of Legal Capacity (Scotland) Act 1991, section 6(2); the law in England, Wales and Northern Ireland does not name a date. As of 16 November 2026, someone born on 29 February 2008 has 105 days to go, to Monday 1 March 2027, when they turn 19. In 2028 the countdown runs to 29 February itself. This is the same rule the main age calculator uses, so the two pages always agree on the date.

Counting down for someone else

Type their date of birth instead of yours. The result shows the age they’re turning, which is handy for the number on the card or the candles, and the weekday, which helps when you’re planning a party around it. If you want the gap between your age and theirs rather than a countdown, the age difference calculator does that.

What the other figures mean

Weeks splits the same wait into whole weeks and leftover days. Weekend birthday? tells you whether the date is a Saturday or Sunday. Birthdays so far is the number of birthdays already had, which is the same as current age in completed years.

To count down to something that isn’t a birthday, such as a wedding, a due date or the end of term, put today and that date into days between dates. It gives the same day count plus weekdays.

Questions people ask

How many days until my birthday?

Type your date of birth above and the big number is the answer, counted from today in your time zone. On the day before your birthday it shows 1.

What day of the week is my birthday this year?

The result names the weekday for your next birthday, and the line “Weekend birthday?” says whether it falls on a Saturday or Sunday.

What if my birthday is 29 February?

This calculator counts a 29 February birthday as 1 March in years without one, so the countdown runs to 1 March. In leap years it runs to the 29th.

Does the countdown change with time zones?

Yes. It counts calendar days in your time zone, so someone on the other side of the world may reach their birthday a day earlier or later than the page shows for you.
