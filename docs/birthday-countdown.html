Birthday countdown

Days until the next birthday, and the day of the week it lands on.

Today

16November

Type a date and the result appears here as you go.

A completed month lands on the same date next month. If that date doesn’t exist — 31 January to 1 March, or a 29 February birthday in a common year — it rolls to the 1st of the next month. Some software, including common date libraries, uses the last day of the month instead, so results can differ by a day around month ends.

How the countdown works

The count is whole days from today to the birthday, so on the day before it says 1. “Until midnight tonight” ticks down the rest of today on your device’s clock. If the birthday is today, the page says so instead of counting to next year.

Born on 29 February? In a year without one, the countdown runs to 1 March, the day this calculator counts you a year older (the rule Scottish law uses).
