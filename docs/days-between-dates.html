How to count the days between dates

Enter a start date and an end date and the page counts the days between dates. The big number is the total. Underneath it you get the same span as years, months and days, then weeks, weekdays (Monday to Friday), weekend days and total months. Dates can be typed in words or numbers, so “25 Dec 2026” and “25/12/2026” both work.

By default the start day is counted and the end day isn’t. That matches how most people count a gap: Monday to Friday is 4 days, the way you’d count nights in a hotel or days until a deadline. Tick “Count the end day too” when both the first and the last day should be included, such as days of annual leave, the days of a festival or a course that runs Monday to Friday. Then Monday to Friday is 5.

A worked example

From Monday 16 November 2026 to Friday 25 December 2026:

  • The rest of November, 16th to 30th, is 15 days (the 16th itself counts, the 25th of December doesn’t).
  • December 1st to 24th is 24 days.
  • 15 + 24 = 39 days, which is 5 weeks and 4 days.

Of those 39 days, 29 are weekdays and 10 fall on a Saturday or Sunday. As years, months and days it is 1 month and 9 days: 16 November to 16 December, then nine more days. Tick “Count the end day too” and the total becomes 40, with 30 weekdays, because Christmas Day 2026 is a Friday.

Weekdays are not working days

The weekday figure counts every Monday to Friday in the span. It doesn’t take out bank holidays, because those differ between England and Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland and every other country, and some years move them. A span that runs through Christmas 2026 will count Friday 25 December and Monday 28 December (the substitute Boxing Day bank holiday) as weekdays. Take bank holidays off by hand if you need an exact number of working days.

Leap years and month lengths

Every date is counted on the real calendar. 1 February to 1 March is 28 days in 2026 and 29 days in 2028, because 2028 is a leap year. A span that includes 29 February is a day longer than the same span in a common year.

The years, months and days line counts months the same way an age is counted. Where the start is the 31st and the following month is shorter, the next whole month is reached on the 1st of the month after: 31 January to 1 March is exactly one month. Common date libraries clamp to the last day of the month instead, so other tools can show a months-and-days figure that differs by a day around month ends.

Counting backwards

You can put the later date first. The total is the same length, and the result carries a note that the end date is before the start and the span has been counted backwards. That is handy for checking how long ago something happened without reordering the boxes.

Days between dates or age difference?

This page measures a period of time: a notice period, a contract, a pregnancy so far, the gap between two holidays. It doesn’t care whether either date is a birthday. To compare two people’s ages, the age difference calculator says who is older and how the gap in their ages changes through the year. For one person’s age on a date, use the age calculator. For the days left until a birthday, the birthday countdown does it with one date.

Days between dates FAQs

Does the count include the end date?

Not unless you tick “Count the end day too”. Without it, the start day counts and the end day doesn’t, so the 1st to the 2nd of a month is 1 day.

How many days is it from today to Christmas?

On 16 November 2026 there were 39 days to go until 25 December 2026. Type today’s date and 25 December to check from any day.

Does the calculator account for leap years?

Yes. Dates are counted on the real calendar, so a span that includes 29 February 2028 is a day longer than the same span in 2026 or 2027.

Can it count working days?

It counts weekdays, Monday to Friday. It doesn’t remove bank holidays, so subtract those yourself.
