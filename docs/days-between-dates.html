Is the end day included?

Not unless you tick the box. From Monday to Friday is 4 days on this page, the way you’d count nights in a hotel or days until a deadline. If you’re counting days of leave or days of a festival where both the first and last day count, tick “Count the end day too” and you’ll get 5.

From 16 November 2026 to 25 December 2026 is 39 days, or 5 weeks and 4 days. 29 of those are weekdays.

Weekdays aren’t working days

The weekday figure counts Monday to Friday. It doesn’t take out bank holidays, because they differ between England, Scotland, Wales, Northern Ireland and every other country. Take them off by hand if your count needs to be exact.

Months and years

The line under the total splits the same stretch into years, months and days, counted the same way as an age. If you want to know how old a person is rather than how long a period lasted, the age calculator is the better page.

Questions people ask

Does the calculator account for leap years?

Yes. Every date is counted on the real calendar, so a span that includes 29 February 2028 is a day longer.

Can the end date be before the start date?

Yes. The result is the same length, counted backwards, and the page tells you it has done that.
