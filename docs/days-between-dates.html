Days between dates

How many days sit between two calendar dates, plus the same span written as years, months and weeks. This is a duration, not a person’s age.

A duration, not an age

If a project started on 15 March 1995 and ended on 16 November 2026, 11,568 days sit between those mornings. That is also 31 years, 8 months and 1 day — the same arithmetic as the age calculator, used here as a length of time. The page leads with the day count because that is what “days between dates” asks for.

The age difference page is the better place if you are comparing two people’s birthdays. This one is for contracts, trip lengths, and “how many days until then” when the two dates are just dates.

Leap days in the span are included in the day total: 28 February 2000 to 1 March 2000 is two days, because 2000 was a leap year; 28 February 1900 to 1 March 1900 is one day, because 1900 was not. Month-end rules are the same as elsewhere: 31 January plus one month is the last day of February, not 3 March.

Questions people actually ask

Does the count include both dates?

The figure is the number of midnights between the two dates. 1 January to 2 January is 1 day. 1 January to 1 January is 0. If you need an inclusive hotel-style count, add one.

Why do you also show years and months?

Because a stretch of 800 days is easier to picture as 2 years, 2 months and a leftover. The heading figure is still the day count, which is the intent of this page.
