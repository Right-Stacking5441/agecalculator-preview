How cat years are counted

A cat’s first year is roughly 15 human years and the second takes them to about 24. After that each year adds around 4. A 10-year-old cat is about 56 in human years; a 15-year-old about 76.

Unlike dogs, cats don’t vary much in size, so one rule fits most of them. Indoor cats tend to live longer than outdoor cats, but that changes how long they live, not how old a given age is.

Life stages

The 2021 feline life stage guidelines from the American Animal Hospital Association and the American Association of Feline Practitioners use four stages: kitten, from birth to a year; young adult, 1 to 6; mature adult, 7 to 10; and senior, over 10. Vets often suggest more frequent check-ups from the senior stage.
