Why not just times seven?

Dogs grow up much faster than people at the start and then slow down, and big dogs age faster than small ones later on. A one-year-old dog can usually breed and is close to fully grown, which is nearer a human teenager than a seven-year-old child.

So this page uses the common size-based rule: the first year counts as 15 human years, the second adds 9, and every year after that adds 4 for a small dog, 5 for a medium one, 6 for a large one and 7 for a giant breed. A five-year-old small dog comes out at 36; a five-year-old large dog at 42.

The DNA-clock estimate

In 2020 a team at the University of California San Diego (Wang and colleagues, in Cell Systems) compared chemical changes in DNA between dogs and people and fitted a formula: human age = 16 × ln(dog age) + 31. It was measured on Labrador retrievers, so treat it as an interesting second opinion for other breeds. It isn’t meant for dogs under a year.

Which size is my dog?

Use the weight they are, or will be, as an adult. A Jack Russell is small, a Border Collie medium, a Labrador large and a Great Dane giant. Crossbreeds go by adult weight.

Questions people ask

How old is a 10-year-old dog in human years?

About 56 for a small dog, 64 for a medium one, 72 for a large dog and 80 for a giant breed, by the size rule on this page.
