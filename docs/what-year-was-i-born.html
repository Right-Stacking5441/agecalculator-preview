Why there are two answers

Someone who is 31 on 16 November 2026 was born in 1995 if they’ve had their birthday this year, or 1994 if it’s still to come in December. Without the birthday you can only narrow it to two years.

Age and birth year in 2026

The first year is for people whose birthday is still to come in 2026; the second for people who have already had it.
