Put in two dates of birth and the age difference calculator shows the gap between them: whole years first, then months, then days. It also says who is older, by how many days in total, and gives the same gap in weeks and months. Names are optional. They only label the result, so “Sam is older by 1,206 days” reads better than “Person A”.

The order you type the dates in doesn’t matter. The calculator works out which birthday came first and counts forward from it.

How the age difference calculator works out the gap

The gap is counted the same way an age is. Start at the earlier date of birth, count the whole years until you would pass the later one, then the whole months, then the days left over.

Take two people born on 2 June 1990 and 20 September 1993:

  • 2 June 1990 to 2 June 1993 is 3 years.
  • 2 June 1993 to 2 September 1993 is 3 months.
  • 2 September to 20 September is 18 days.

So the older one is 3 years, 3 months and 18 days older. Counted in days, that is 1,206, or 172 weeks and 2 days.

Why their ages don’t always differ by the same number

The 1,206 days never change. The difference you’d say out loud does. Between 2 June and 19 September each year, the older person has had their birthday and the younger one hasn’t, so their ages are 4 apart. As of 16 November 2026, for example, they are 36 and 33, which is 3 apart. On 1 August 2027 they will be 37 and 33.

Both answers are right, which is why the result has a line saying the ages differ by “3 or 4, depending on the time of year”. If you only subtract one age from another, you get whichever of the two happens to apply today. The years, months and days figure is the one that stays put.

If the two people share a birthday (same day and month, different years), the gap is a whole number of years and the line says it holds all year.

Leap days and month ends

This calculator counts a 29 February birthday as falling on 1 March in common years, the same rule as the rest of the site. Months are counted on the real calendar too, so a gap that starts on 31 January reaches its first whole month on 1 March, because February has no 31st. Common date libraries clamp to the last day of the month instead, so other tools can differ by a day around month ends.

Twins and same-day births

Twins born either side of midnight have different dates of birth, and the calculator will show them as 1 day apart. If you type the same date twice, it says so rather than showing a gap of zero years, zero months and zero days.

Other things people use it for

Couples comparing ages, siblings settling an argument, and anyone filling in a form that asks for the age gap between a parent and a child. For the span between any two dates that aren’t birthdays, such as the start and end of a tenancy, days between dates is the better tool, because it can count the end day and gives a weekday total. For one person’s age on a given day, use the main age calculator.

Questions people ask

Is the age difference the same every year?

In days, yes. In whole years it changes by one for part of each year, in the weeks between the older person’s birthday and the younger person’s.

How do I work out an age difference by hand?

Subtract the earlier date of birth from the later one: count whole years, then whole months, then the remaining days. 2 June 1990 to 20 September 1993 is 3 years, 3 months and 18 days.

Can I compare more than two people?

Not on one screen. Work out each pair, or compare everyone with the eldest.

Are the names I type saved?

No. The calculation runs in your browser, and the names are only used to label the result on your screen.
