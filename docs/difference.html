What “age difference” means here

If one person was born on 15 March 1995 and the other on 1 January 1990, the younger one is 5 years, 2 months and 14 days younger. That is an age gap, not a hotel stay and not a countdown. The arithmetic is the same civil count as the homepage, including 1 March for a 29 February birthday in a common year, and the January 31 month-end clamp.

The live app already had a /difference path, and it sat at about position 9 for “age calculator difference” — then lost the click because every page on the app shared the same title. This page has its own title and meta, and it should keep the /difference slug when the root site eventually replaces the app host.

If you only need how many days sit between two dates, use days between dates. If you want one person’s age on a date in the past, use How old was I?.

Questions people actually ask

Is this the same as days between dates?

No. This page is the gap between two dates of birth, written as years, months and days — “four years and two months older”. Days between dates leads with the raw day count, for durations that are not someone’s age.

What if I enter the later date first?

The calculator puts the two dates in order and says so. The gap is the same either way.
