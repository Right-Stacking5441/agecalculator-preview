Type a date and the result appears here as you go.

Type a date of birth in the box and this age calculator gives your age as completed years, then months, then days, worked out to today’s date in your time zone. The big number is your age as you’d write it on a form. The line under it is the months and days since your last birthday. Below that, the same stretch of time is given in single units: total months, weeks, days and hours.

The box reads dates the way people type them: 15 March 1995, 15/03/1995, 1995-03-15 or 15031995 all work, and the line underneath says how it has read your entry. If a date could go either way, like 1/2/2000, it follows your browser’s language setting and offers the other reading with one tap.

How this age calculator counts

Your age goes up by one on your birthday, the same way it does on a passport or a school form. The calculator counts whole years first, then the whole months left over, then the days left after that.

The single-unit figures don’t add on to your age. 380 months and 1,652 weeks are two ways of measuring the same span, not extra time. Hours are the number of days times 24, counted from midnight on the day you were born, because a date of birth on its own doesn’t record what time you arrived. Clock changes aren’t counted.

A worked example: 15 March 1995

As of 16 November 2026, someone born on 15 March 1995 is 31 years, 8 months and 1 day old. On paper:

  • 2026 minus 1995 is 31, and their birthday on 15 March 2026 has already passed, so 31 completed years.
  • 15 March to 15 November is 8 whole months.
  • 15 November to 16 November is 1 day.

That is 11,569 days in total. They were born on a Wednesday, and they turn 32 on Monday 15 March 2027, 119 days away. If this year’s birthday is still to come, take one off the years and count the months from last year’s birthday instead.

Born on 29 February

A 29 February birthday only exists in leap years, such as 2024, 2028 and 2032. In the other three years, this calculator counts a 29 February birthday as falling on 1 March, the day after 28 February, and every tool on this site uses that rule. It is the rule written into Scottish law by the Age of Legal Capacity (Scotland) Act 1991, section 6(2). The law in England, Wales and Northern Ireland does not name a date, so if a particular contract or document says something else, go by its wording.

Take someone born on 29 February 2000. There was no 29 February in 2026, so their 26th birthday counted on 1 March 2026. As of 16 November 2026, they are 26 years, 8 months and 18 days old. The whole months are counted on the 29th, the day they were born, so the eighth one after the 26th birthday ends on 29 October 2026, and from 29 October to 16 November is 18 days. Their next birthday counts on Monday 1 March 2027. In 2028 it is back on the 29th.

The same rule covers month ends. There is no 31 February, so a month after 31 January is 1 March here. Plenty of people born on a leap day celebrate on 28 February, and that’s fine for a party. The calculator just doesn’t treat it as the day the age changes.

Which day counts as today

The calculator works with calendar dates, not clock times. It takes today’s date from the time zone your device reports and shows that zone beside the date. When it is already Tuesday in London it can still be Monday in Los Angeles, so on the day of a birthday the answer depends on where you are. Press Change to pick another zone, or to type a different “age on” date altogether.

Other ways of counting age

South Korea made international age, the kind shown here, the default in Korean law from 28 June 2023. Two older systems are still around there: Korean age, where you are 1 at birth and everyone gets a year older on 1 January, and year age, which is simply this year minus your birth year. Korean age is still heard in everyday life, and a few rules, such as buying alcohol and tobacco and military service, used year age when the change came in. Tick Show Korean age to add it to your result, or use the Korean age calculator to see all three side by side. That page explains the counting. It isn’t legal advice.

Schools, speech and language therapists and psychologists usually want a child’s age on the day of a test, written as years;months;days, for example 8;4;12. Your result shows that line as well. The chronological age calculator is built for the job, with a test date field, a printable slip and a class sheet. If a test manual has its own rounding rule, follow the manual.

Questions people ask

How old am I if I was born on 15 March 1995?

As of 16 November 2026, you are 31 years, 8 months and 1 day old, and you turn 32 on Monday 15 March 2027. For any other day, press Change and type it in.

If I was born on 29 February, when do I turn a year older?

This calculator counts it as 1 March in years with no 29 February, and the 29th itself in leap years. That matches Scottish law; the law in England, Wales and Northern Ireland does not name a date.

Does this use Korean age or international age?

International age: you start at 0 and add a year on each birthday. Tick Show Korean age to see the traditional count beside it.

Can I see my age on a date in the past or the future?

Yes. Press Change and type any date. There are also separate pages for how old you were on a past date and how old you will be in a future year.

Why is my age in months and days sometimes surprising at the end of a month?

Months aren’t all the same length. If you were born on 31 January, there is no 31 February, so your first whole month is counted on 1 March, the same rule used for 29 February. On 28 February the result shows 0 months and 28 days; on 1 March it shows exactly 1 month. Common date libraries clamp to the last day of the month instead (31 January plus a month becomes 28 February), so other tools can differ by a day around month ends.
