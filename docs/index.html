How this calculator counts age

Your age goes up by one on your birthday, the way it does on a passport or a school form. The calculator counts the completed years first, then the whole months left over, then the days left after that. That’s the big figure and the line under it in the result.

The list underneath (months, weeks, days and hours) is the same stretch of time measured in a single unit, so those numbers don’t add on to your age. Hours are counted from midnight, because a date of birth on its own doesn’t say what time you arrived.

A worked example: 15 March 1995

The example in the box is 15 March 1995. As of 16 November 2026, someone born that day is 31 years, 8 months and 1 day old. On paper it goes like this:

2026 − 1995, and 15 March 2026 has already been31 years
15 March to 15 November8 months
15 November to 16 November1 day
Age on 16 November 202631 y 8 m 1 d

If this year’s birthday hasn’t come round yet, take one off the years and count the months from last year’s birthday instead.

Born on 29 February

A 29 February birthday only exists in leap years: 2024, 2028, 2032 and so on. In the other three years this calculator counts you a year older on 1 March, the day after 28 February. That’s the rule written into Scottish law. Elsewhere in the UK the law doesn’t name a date, and some places and rules use 28 February instead. Every result on this site uses that rule. If a particular law or contract says otherwise, go by that.

Take someone born on 29 February 2000. Their 26th birthday counted on 1 March 2026, because 2026 has no 29 February. On 16 November 2026 they are 26 years, 8 months and 18 days old: eight months takes them to 29 October, and 18 more days to the 16th. Their next birthday counts on Monday 1 March 2027, and in 2028 it’s back on the 29th.

Plenty of people born on the day celebrate on 28 February instead. That’s fine for a party. The calculator just doesn’t treat it as the day the age changes.

Which day is “today”?

The calculator works with calendar dates, not clock times, and takes today’s date from the time zone your device reports. The zone is shown next to the date. When it’s already Tuesday in London it can still be Monday in Los Angeles, so on the day of a birthday the answer depends on where you are. To use a different zone, press Change under the date of birth and pick one.

Other ways of counting age

South Korea has used international age, the kind shown here, for legal and official purposes since 28 June 2023. Two older systems are still common in everyday life: Korean age, where you’re 1 at birth and everyone gets a year older on 1 January, and year age, which is this year minus your birth year. Tick Show Korean age to add it to your result, or use the Korean age calculator to see all three side by side. That page explains the counting; it isn’t legal advice.

Schools, speech and language therapists and psychologists often need a child’s age on the day of a test, written as years;months;days, like 8;4;12. Your result shows that line too. The chronological age calculator is built for the job, with a test date field and a slip you can print for the file. If the test manual has its own rounding rule, the manual wins.

Questions people ask

How old am I if I was born on 15 March 1995?

On 16 November 2026 you’re 31 years, 8 months and 1 day old, and you turn 32 on Monday 15 March 2027. For a different day, press Change next to “Age on” and type it in.

If I was born on 29 February, when do I turn a year older?

On 1 March in years that have no 29 February, and on the 29th itself in leap years. That’s how this calculator counts, and it’s what Scottish law says: in a year without 29 February, the birthday is taken to be 1 March. The law in England, Wales and Northern Ireland doesn’t name a date, and some places and rules use 28 February. If a particular law or contract says otherwise, go by that.

Does this use Korean age or international age?

International age: you start at 0 and add a year on each birthday. South Korea has used it for official purposes since June 2023. Tick Show Korean age to see the traditional count as well.

Can I see my age on a date in the past or the future?

Yes. Press Change next to “Age on” and type any date. There are also pages for how old you were on a past date and how old you’ll be in a future year.

How does this calculator count a month, or a 29 February birthday?

A completed month lands on the same date next month. If that date doesn’t exist — there is no 31 February, and some years have no 29 February — it rolls to the 1st of the next month, so 31 January plus one month is 1 March, and a 29 February birthday counts on 1 March in a common year. Common date libraries (for example date-fns, Python’s dateutil and Java’s LocalDate) use the last day of the month instead, so around month ends other tools can differ by a day or two.
