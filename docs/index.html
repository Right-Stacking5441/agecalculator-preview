Working it out by hand: 15 March 1995

On 16 November 2026, for example, someone born on 15 March 1995 is 31 years, 8 months and 1 day old. On paper:

  • 2026 minus 1995 is 31, and their birthday on 15 March 2026 has already passed, so 31 completed years.
  • 15 March to 15 November is 8 whole months.
  • 15 November to 16 November is 1 day.

That is 11,569 days in total. They were born on a Wednesday, and their 32nd birthday, on Monday 15 March 2027, is 119 days away. If the birthday in the year you’re checking hasn’t come yet, take one off the years and count the months from the birthday the year before.

What the result shows

Your age comes out as completed years, then months, then days, worked out to today’s date in your time zone. The big number is your age as you’d write it on a form. The line under it is the months and days since your last birthday. Below that, one line gives the same stretch of time in weeks and in days, with hours under More.

The box reads dates the way people type them: 15 March 1995, 15/03/1995, 1995-03-15 or 15031995 all work, and the line underneath says how it has read your entry. If a date could go either way, like 1/2/2000, it follows your browser’s language setting and offers the other reading with one tap.

How your age is counted

Your age goes up by one on your birthday, the same way it does on a passport or a school form. The age calculator counts whole years first, then the whole months left over, then the days left after that.

The single-unit figures don’t add on to your age. 380 months and 1,652 weeks are two ways of measuring the same span, not extra time. Hours are the number of days times 24, counted from midnight on the day you were born, because a date of birth on its own doesn’t record what time you arrived. Clock changes aren’t counted.

Born on 29 February

A 29 February birthday only exists in leap years, such as 2024, 2028 and 2032. In the other three years, this calculator counts a 29 February birthday as falling on 1 March, the day after 28 February, and every tool on this site uses that rule. It is the rule written into Scottish law by the Age of Legal Capacity (Scotland) Act 1991, section 6(2). The law in England, Wales and Northern Ireland does not name a date, so if a particular contract or document says something else, go by its wording.

Worked example, on 16 November 2026: take someone born on 29 February 2000. There is no 29 February in 2026, so their 26th birthday counts on 1 March 2026. On 16 November 2026 they are 26 years, 8 months and 18 days old. The whole months are counted on the 29th, the day they were born, so the eighth one after the 26th birthday ends on 29 October 2026, and from 29 October to 16 November is 18 days. Their 27th birthday counts on Monday 1 March 2027. In 2028 it is back on the 29th.

The same rule covers month ends. There is no 31 February, so a month after 31 January is 1 March here. Plenty of people born on a leap day celebrate on 28 February, and that’s fine for a party. The calculator just doesn’t treat it as the day the age changes.

Which day counts as today

The calculator works with calendar dates, not clock times. It takes today’s date from the time zone your device reports and shows that zone beside the date. When it is already Tuesday in London it can still be Monday in Los Angeles, so on the day of a birthday the answer depends on where you are. Press Change to pick another zone, or to type a different “age on” date altogether.

Other ways of counting age

South Korea made international age, the kind shown here, the default in Korean law from 28 June 2023. Two older systems are still around there: Korean age, where you are 1 at birth and everyone gets a year older on 1 January, and year age, which is simply this year minus your birth year. Korean age is still heard in everyday life, and a few rules, such as buying alcohol and tobacco and military service, used year age when the change came in. Your Korean age is under More in your result, or use the Korean age calculator to see all three side by side. That page explains the counting. It isn’t legal advice.

Schools, speech and language therapists and psychologists usually want a child’s age on the day of a test, written as years;months;days, for example 8;4;12. Your result shows that line too, under More. The chronological age calculator is built for the job, with a test date field, a printable slip and a class sheet. If a test manual has its own rounding rule, follow the manual.

More on how it works

Ages on past or future dates

Any date works, earlier or later than today: press Change and type it. There’s also a separate page for how old you will be in a future year, which gives the date you turn that age as well.

Ages at the end of a month

Someone born on 31 January shows as 0 months and 28 days old on 28 February, and exactly 1 month old on 1 March, because there is no 31 February. Some other calculators end that month on 28 February instead, so they can be a day out around month ends.
