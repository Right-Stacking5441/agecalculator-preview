Cat age calculator

Estimate a cat’s age in human years with the usual 15, then 24, then four-years-at-a-time curve — different from the dog table.

Estimate

The curve this page uses

A widely used cat scale, and the one implemented here, is: year one is about 15 human years, year two brings the total to 24, and each year after that adds 4. A six-year-old cat is about 40 human years on that curve. There is no size-group split on this page, which is one reason it should not be copied from the dog age calculator.

This is an estimate used for a rough comparison, not a veterinary assessment, and not a claim about any one breed. Times seven is the wrong shortcut for cats as well as dogs: it undershoots the early years and does not match the later ones either.
