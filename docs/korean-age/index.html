The three counts, with a date on them

International age is the usual civil age used on this site: it goes up on the birthday. A person born on 15 March 1995 is 31 in international age on 16 November 2026.

Year age, sometimes called yeon nai, is the current calendar year minus the birth year. For that same person it is also 31 in 2026. A person born on 15 December 1995 is only 30 in international age on 16 November 2026, but their year age is already 31, because 2026 − 1995 = 31.

Traditional Korean age, or se, is 1 at birth and then increases by one every New Year (1 January, in modern practice). It is year age plus one. The March 1995 birth is 32 se on 16 November 2026; the December 1995 birth is also 32 se.

From 28 June 2023, South Korea uses international age for legal and administrative purposes. Se and year age are still used in conversation. This page states the three numbers; it is not a source for Korean civil law.

Questions people actually ask

Which age is legal in South Korea now?

International age, from June 2023. Traditional Korean age (se) and year age remain social systems. This page is not legal advice.
