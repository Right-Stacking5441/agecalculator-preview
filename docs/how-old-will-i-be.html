Two ages in one year

Everyone is two ages during a year: one before their birthday and one after. Someone born on 15 March 1995 will be 54 at the start of 2050 and turn 55 on Tuesday 15 March 2050. The result shows both, and the date the number changes.

If you need the age on a particular day, such as a retirement date or the day a policy ends, press Change and type that date in.
