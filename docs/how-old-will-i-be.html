How old will I be?

Exact age on a date you have not reached yet.

Pick a date of birth and a date still to come — 1 January 2050 is filled in as a starting point — and see the exact age on that morning.

Starts at 1 January 2050; change it to any later day.

Age on a day that has not happened yet

The count is the same civil age as the homepage, just pointed at a later date. Someone born on 15 March 1995 will be 54 years, 9 months and 17 days old on 1 January 2050: 2050 − 1995 = 55, but 15 March has not yet arrived, so that is 54 completed years, then nine months from 15 March 2049 to 15 December 2049, then 17 days to 1 January.

A person born on 29 February 2000 will be 49 years, 10 months and 1 day old on 1 January 2050, because their civil anniversary in 2049, a common year, is 1 March.

This is not a forecast of anything except the calendar. For a date already in the past, use How old was I?. For the wait until the next birthday, use the birthday countdown.
